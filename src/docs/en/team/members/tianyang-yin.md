## About

Tianyang is a PhD student in Civil Engineering at McMaster University. Tianyang's previous research examined the durability and structural performance of reinforced-concrete and steel–concrete composite beams under salt freeze–thaw cycles, fatigue, and contact explosion loading.

## Research interests

- bridge and structural engineering;
- durability of reinforced-concrete and composite structures;
- freeze–thaw and fatigue deterioration; and
- structural response under extreme loading.

## Education

- PhD in Civil Engineering, McMaster University, 2025–present
- Graduate studies, Chang'an University, 2021-2024
