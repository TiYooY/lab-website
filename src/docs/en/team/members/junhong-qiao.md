## About

Junhong will join the lab as a PhD student in September 2026. Junhong completed a master's degree project in Structural Engineering and Bridges at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, studying fatigue behaviour of steel piles in integral bridges under traffic and temperature loading.

## Research interests

- integral bridge behaviour;
- fatigue of steel piles;
- bridge–soil interaction; and
- finite element and parametric analysis.
