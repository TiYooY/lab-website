## About

Dr. Cancan Yang is an associate professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at McMaster University and the principal investigator of CAN Lab. Her research connects structural engineering, large-scale experimentation, advanced concrete materials, and computational analysis to improve infrastructure performance, durability, resilience, and sustainability.

## Research interests

- **Structural sustainability:** reuse-aware design of bridge and building systems, assessment of reclaimed components and connections, and structural performance across multiple service lives.
- **Adaptive bridge design:** bridge response to autonomous truck platooning, changing transportation demands, and climate-informed loading and deterioration scenarios.
- **Bridge life-cycle and risk assessment:** durability prediction, multi-hazard performance, reliability, and risk-informed maintenance and design decisions.
- **3D concrete printing:** printable high-performance concrete, structural behaviour of printed components, and design and testing methods for additive construction.

Learn more on the lab's [Research](../../research.md) and [Publications](../../publications.md) pages.
