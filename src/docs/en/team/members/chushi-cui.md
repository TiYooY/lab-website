## About

Chushi will join the lab as a PhD student in September 2026. Chushi's previous research at Southeast University focused on ultra-high-performance concrete structural members reinforced or strengthened with self-prestressed iron-based shape-memory alloys.

## Research interests

- ultra-high-performance concrete structures;
- iron-based shape-memory alloys;
- self-prestressed reinforcement and strengthening; and
- experimental behaviour of composite columns.

## Education

- PhD in Civil Engineering, McMaster University, beginning September 2026
- Previous graduate research affiliation, Southeast University
