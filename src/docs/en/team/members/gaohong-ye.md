## About

Gaohong is a PhD student in Civil Engineering at McMaster University. Current work examines corrosion and axial performance of buried steel piles using field data, structural analysis, and comparisons with international design and soil-corrosivity standards.

## Research interests

- corrosion of buried steel structures;
- axial behaviour and stability of steel piles;
- soil–structure interaction; and
- field-data analysis and design-code assessment.

## Education

- PhD in Civil Engineering, McMaster University, 2024–present
- Master of Engineering
