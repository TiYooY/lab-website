## About

Ali is a PhD student in Civil Engineering at McMaster University. Ali's research investigates how truck platooning affects flexible pavement performance and supports the design of transportation infrastructure for emerging vehicle technologies.

## Research interests

- pavement engineering;
- truck platooning and heavy vehicle loading;
- pavement cracking and damage assessment; and
- transportation infrastructure performance.

## Education

- PhD in Civil Engineering, McMaster University, 2023–present
