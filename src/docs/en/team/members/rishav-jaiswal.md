## About

Rishav is a PhD candidate in Civil Engineering at McMaster University. His research examines the resilience of highway bridges and transportation networks under flood, scour, corrosion, and extreme traffic loading. His work combines finite element analysis, structural reliability and risk assessment, hydraulic and corrosion modelling, and machine learning.

## Research interests

- climate-resilient highway infrastructure;
- bridge scour, corrosion, and multi-hazard risk;
- finite element and surrogate modelling; and
- machine learning for structural and transportation systems.
